The plot

A 60 × 40 m yard on the south bank of the Danube, where the river's low gravel terrace drops to the floodplain. Four trenches and five boreholes. Trench 2 holds the full sequence and is the drawing on the Section page.

The report series

Each report is a full record: method, results tables, drawings and interpretation, cross-linked to the layers they describe. Start with RN-01 for the setting or RN-12 for the whole story.
