Regensburg · old town · excavated 1995–1998

Neupfarrplatz

Under one square in Regensburg lie 2,000 years: Roman officers' houses, the medieval Jewish quarter and its synagogue, the rubble of 1519 and a wartime bunker. Twelve study reports on what the excavation found and what the ground holds.

What lies under the square

The Roman remains lie about six metres down. Above them the cellars of the Jewish quarter survived because, when the quarter was destroyed in 1519, they were filled in rather than dug out. A concrete escape gallery of 1939/40 runs straight through them.1821

The reports

Each report gathers what the published sources say on one topic, with tables and drawings, and says plainly what is not published online. Start with NP-02 for the dig itself or NP-03 for the layers.
