The city and the square

Timeline

What happened in Regensburg as a whole, beside what happened on the ground of Neupfarrplatz. Every entry is real and links to its source.

WhenRegensburgNeupfarrplatz
c. 160 million years ago
Regensburg

A last wide Late Jurassic flooding spreads the sea far over the basement east of the city.1

Cretaceous
Regensburg

The Cenomanian sea pushes in from the south and the Grünsandstein is laid down; the sea leaves in the Santonian and the area stays land.17

Last glaciation
Regensburg

The Danube builds its low gravel terraces NT1 to NT3.4

AD 179
Regensburg

The legionary fortress Castra Regina is built for Legio III Italica under Emperor Marcus Aurelius.10

Neupfarrplatz

The ground of the future square lies inside the camp; in antiquity and late antiquity it holds Roman officers' residences.19

After 900
Neupfarrplatz

Duke Arnulf I has the camp's west wall, which crossed the square, pulled down and builds a new wall about 500 m further west.12

981
Neupfarrplatz

First evidence of a Jewish community in Regensburg.1219

c. 1000
Neupfarrplatz

The community has a synagogue, a school, a court and a cemetery.15

1020
Neupfarrplatz

The Jewish quarter on today's square is first documented.16

Mid-11th century
Neupfarrplatz

The first synagogue is built; it is badly damaged by fire in the next century.22

1135–1146
Regensburg

The Stone Bridge is built across the Danube.8

After 1215
Neupfarrplatz

Jews may live only inside the walled area of today's square.15

Early 13th century
Neupfarrplatz

A new synagogue replaces the smaller first one;13 one source dates its completion to 1227.15

1245
Regensburg

Regensburg becomes a Free Imperial City.10

1273
Regensburg

Building of the Gothic cathedral begins.8

July 1342
Regensburg

The Magdalene flood strikes the Rhine, Main, Danube and other rivers; Regensburg's chronicles record it.9

Around 1388
Neupfarrplatz

624 gold coins are buried in two clay pots in one of the cellars.16

12 January 1519
Regensburg

Emperor Maximilian I, protector of the Jews, dies in Wels.13

21 February 1519
Neupfarrplatz

The council orders the expulsion of about 500 people; demolition of the synagogue begins the same day.1316

April – September 1519
Neupfarrplatz

A wooden pilgrimage chapel is consecrated in April; on 19 September the foundation stone of a stone church is laid, built partly with stones from the Jewish houses and gravestones.14

1528
Neupfarrplatz

Work on the church stops for lack of money.12

1540 – 1542
Regensburg

Regensburg turns Lutheran in 1542.12

Neupfarrplatz

The unfinished church is consecrated in 1540 and becomes the first Protestant parish church, the Neupfarrkirche.14

1860
Neupfarrplatz

A new west choir by Ludwig Foltz finally closes the church.12

1939/40
Neupfarrplatz

A ring-shaped escape gallery and a fire-water cistern are built under the square, cutting through the medieval cellars.1221

Early 1970s
Neupfarrplatz

22 old houses on the east and south sides are pulled down for a department store.12

1995–1998
Neupfarrplatz

The excavation: about 3,000 m², the synagogue and its predecessor, the cellars of the Jewish quarter and the gold hoard.11

2005
Neupfarrplatz

Dani Karavan's memorial Misrach traces the synagogue's plan on the square (some sources say 2004).1216

2006
Regensburg

The Old Town of Regensburg with Stadtamhof becomes a UNESCO World Heritage Site.10

2010
Regensburg

Restoration of the Stone Bridge begins.8

2019
Neupfarrplatz

An LMU project funded by the DFG and the city begins the full analysis of the excavation.20
